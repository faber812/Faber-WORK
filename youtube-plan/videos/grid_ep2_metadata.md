# Shorts #2: метаданные для загрузки

**Название (title):**
The US Grid Is Still Down — Hour 6 ⚡

**Описание (description):**
Hour 6 of a nationwide blackout: your fridge crosses the line, cell towers start going dark, and every intersection becomes a stop sign.

Missed Hour 1? Watch it first. Hour 12 is next. Follow for part three.

Sources:
• FoodSafety.gov, "Food Safety During Power Outage" (discard perishables above 40°F for 2+ hrs; full freezer ~48 hrs, half full ~24 hrs): https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-safety-charts/food-safety-during-power-outage
• USDA FSIS, "Keep Your Food Safe During Emergencies": https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/emergencies/keep-your-food-safe-during-emergencies
• CPUC decision on telecom backup power (industry guidelines: min. 4 hrs): https://docs.cpuc.ca.gov/published/FINAL_DECISION/87681-06.htm
• Sewerage & Water Board of New Orleans: a water tower holds roughly one day's supply.
