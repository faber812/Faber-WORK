# Shorts #3: метаданные для загрузки

**Название (title):**
Why Your Phone Dies in a Blackout — Hour 12 ⚡

**Описание (description):**
Hour 12 of a nationwide blackout. Your Wi-Fi is gone, cell towers are running on backup, and your phone is burning battery just to stay connected.

What the FCC and FEMA recommend: keep calls short, and try texting. A text can get through when a call can't.

Part 4 (Hour 24) is next: your water. Follow so you don't miss it.

Sources:
• FCC/FEMA, "Emergency Communications Tips": https://www.fcc.gov/emergency
• CPUC decision on telecom backup power (industry guidelines: min. 4 hrs): https://docs.cpuc.ca.gov/published/FINAL_DECISION/87681-06.htm
