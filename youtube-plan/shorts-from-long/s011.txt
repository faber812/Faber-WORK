Your power's dead. Your car's stuck. The garage door won't budge. How do you get it out? It probably isn't locked. It's hooked to an opener with no power. So you unhook it.

Look up at the opener's rail. A red cord hangs down. It's called the emergency release. Opener makers say pull it only with the door fully closed, if you can. The springs do the heavy lifting.

But if one is weak or broken, the door can drop fast. So look at the springs first. If one looks broken, don't pull. Call a garage door pro. Clear people and pets from the doorway.

If the door is stuck open, use caution and keep everyone out from under it. If it's closed, pull the cord down. Lift by the door, never the cord, hands off the hinges.

A balanced door lifts smoothly. If it feels really heavy, won't stay up, or drops, stop and call a pro. Now you can drive out. Door closed, springs okay, pull the cord, lift by the door.
