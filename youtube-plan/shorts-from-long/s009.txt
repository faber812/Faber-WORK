Don't run your generator in the garage. Not even with the door open. This is the mistake that kills people on day two of a blackout.

Day two. The freezer is giving up, the house is cold, so people get careful. They don't run it inside the house. They put it in the garage... and crack the door.

And here's why that still kills. Carbon monoxide has no smell. No color. It doesn't make you cough. It drifts from the garage into the house.

The first signs are a headache... dizziness... feeling tired. In the dark, on day two, that feels normal. So people lie down.

The CDC counts more than four hundred deaths a year in the US from carbon monoxide that has nothing to do with fires. And it says: don't even run a car in an attached garage with the door open.

The rule: generators, grills and camp stoves go outside. At least twenty feet from doors and windows. And put a battery-powered CO alarm in your home. It's the only thing that will warn you.
