## Тексты неозвученных частей

**День 5**
```
[calm, serious] Day five. The gas in your tank is running low... and the stations still can't pump.

Most stores depend on constant deliveries. Without fuel and power, the shelves stay empty.

And there's a problem almost nobody thinks about. Sewage. Sewer systems rely on electric pumps called lift stations. FEMA warns that in a long outage, sewage levels rise... and can eventually back up.

Cash is the only payment that still works. If you have any.
```

**День 7**
```
[calm, serious] Day seven. One week. Hospitals are still running... on generators. Many are expected to plan for about ninety-six hours on their own. After that, everything depends on fuel deliveries.

So how do you restart a dead grid? It's called a black start. A few special power plants can start with no outside power. They energize one line... wake up the next plant... and rebuild the grid, piece by piece.

It takes time. In two thousand three, a blackout hit about fifty million people in the US and Canada. Some areas waited four days for power.
```

## Источники новых частей

- **Канализация:** FEMA, «Reducing Loss of Utility Impacts to Critical Facilities» — при долгом отключении насосных станций уровень стоков растёт.
- **Больницы, 96 часов:** требование CMS (42 CFR 482.15) и стандарт Joint Commission о планировании автономной работы. Проверено по вторичным источникам, первоисточник перед публикацией стоит открыть.
- **Black start:** NERC / FERC, PNNL-32773 «Electric Grid Blackstart».
- **Блэкаут 2003 года:** U.S.-Canada Power System Outage Task Force, Final Report — около 50 млн человек, в части США электричество восстанавливали до 4 дней.
- **Финал:** Ready.gov, «Build a Kit».
- **Не проверено:** «магазины зависят от постоянных поставок» — общее утверждение без цифры, точного источника нет.
